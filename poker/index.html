Table log · why each computer acted
No-limit Texas hold'em. The button moves one seat left each hand; the two seats after it post the blinds (heads up, the button posts the small blind and acts first before the flop). A raise must be at least the size of the last bet or raise, and all-ins build side pots. Each player makes the best five-card hand from their hole cards plus the board, any five in any mix. With three or more hole cards you can switch on the Omaha rule instead: a hand must use exactly two hole cards and exactly three board cards, so four hearts in your hand with one on the board is no flush. The computer seats and the coach use the Monte Carlo strategy from poker/strategy.py: deal the unseen cards at random many times, measure the share of the pot your hand wins, and compare it with the pot odds. One addition reads the betting. An opponent who has called, bet or raised this hand is dealt only playable hands (a pair, high cards or a draw), and one who has bet or raised twice only strong ones (a good pair or top pair and better). The computer seats also have styles: tight ones fold sooner, loose ones call wider, aggressive ones raise and bluff more.
