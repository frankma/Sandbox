    Practice rules: two packs plus four jokers, 27 cards each, level card above A, heart level cards wild. Climb +3 / +2 / +1 for a 1st-and-2nd / 1st-and-3rd / 1st-and-4th finish. Tribute and return (10 or lower), resisted with both red jokers, and 接风 when the trick winner is already out. To win the match, a team on A must win a hand at A with a 1st-and-2nd or 1st-and-3rd finish; three failures at A drop it to 2. The computer players and the coach share one engine. It splits a hand into the fewest plays, values jokers, level cards and bombs as control, and weighs passing against how close each opponent is to going out. New to the game? Read the GuanDan Primer.
